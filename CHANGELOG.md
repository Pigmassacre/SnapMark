# SnapMark

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Hold a key, flick toward a raid marker, release. It picks by direction, so it never misses.
- Marks your mouseover, or your target. In combat it marks your target, or optionally targets your mouseover first.
- Pick the marker a mob already has to clear it. Release in the centre: nothing, skull, or clear.
- Ground markers on a second key, with the same wheel: release, then click the ground.
- Settings in Options > AddOns > SnapMark, or `/snapmark`.
